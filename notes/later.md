# Later (named, not built)

Things we are not building in the backbone. Named so they have a home and do not sneak into v1.

## Uncertainty (VVUQ, the U)

Multiple seeds, parameter ranges, error bars. First build: a run card plus an automated checklist.

## Ancillary services (AS)

Separate ERCOT products besides energy: regulation, responsive reserve, ECRS, non-spin, and the rest. A fleet can get paid for these. Same *shape* as other interval series, later. First build settles energy SPP only (energy + scarcity + congestion + losses).

## Users

A location could later link to a customer. Not now.

## Comms delay and drop

HQ is always a little behind. Offline is freshness past a cutoff. First build: linked or not, no lag model.

## Feeders, DAM, power flow, mid-run unit moves

Finer grid than substation. Day-ahead vs real-time. Full network. Trucks moving batteries during a run. Not now.
